## Why we are asking you
You are invited because you are a clinician qualified to outline [[ANATOMY OR LESION TYPE]] on [[IMAGING MODALITY]] images. Taking part is voluntary.

## What the study is about
We are testing whether an artificial intelligence model that outlines structures in medical images can learn from the corrections clinicians make, and whether the improved model saves another clinician time without lowering the quality of the final outlines.

## What you will do
- Review [[NUMBER]] de-identified images, each with an outline proposed by the model.
- Correct the outline with a few clicks, or draw it yourself when clicks are not enough, and accept it or flag the case.
- Rate your confidence in each accepted outline and how difficult the case was.
- Expect about [[HOURS]] hours in total, which you can split across several sittings.

## What we record
The site records your clicks and their positions, the outlines you accept, the time you spend (with pauses excluded), your ratings and decisions, and the date and time of each action. Images are de-identified before they reach this site. We do not collect your clinical records or any patient information from you.

## Risks and benefits
We expect no risk beyond that of ordinary computer work and the loss of confidentiality of your study data, which we protect as described below. There is no direct benefit to you. The study may help build tools that reduce annotation effort for clinicians.

## Confidentiality
Your data are stored under a coded participant ID in an access-controlled research database. Your name is kept only with your signed documents and is not published. Reports will describe results for the group of participants. Your coded timing and decision data may be shared with other researchers in de-identified form: [[DATA SHARING STATEMENT]].
