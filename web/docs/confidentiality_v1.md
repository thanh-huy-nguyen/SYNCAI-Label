# Data confidentiality and acceptable use agreement

**Study:** [[STUDY TITLE]] (IRB protocol [[IRB PROTOCOL NUMBER]])

By signing, I agree to the following rules while I take part in this study.

## Images and patient privacy
- The images in this study are de-identified. I will not try to identify any patient from an image, and I will not combine the images with any other source of information about a patient.
- I will not enter patient names, medical record numbers, dates of birth or other direct identifiers anywhere on this site, including in any text field.
- If I believe an image contains identifying information, I will flag the case and tell the study team at once.

## Keeping the data inside the study
- I will not copy, photograph, screenshot, record, download or share any image, outline or model output from this site.
- I will not use any image or outline from this study for any purpose other than completing my assigned cases.
- I will not look at reference outlines, other models' outputs or other participants' work, and I will not discuss my cases or outlines with other participants until the study team says the data collection is finished.

## My account
- I will keep my password private and will not let anyone else use my account.
- I will work on a computer I control, in a place where others cannot see the images.
- I will sign out when I leave the computer, and I will tell the study team if I think my account has been used by someone else.

## Reporting problems
I will report any suspected privacy problem, technical fault that changes my results, or unclear instruction to [[STUDY CONTACT EMAIL]].

## Consequences
I understand that the study team can suspend my access if I break these rules, and that a breach may have to be reported to the institutional review board.

I am signing electronically and agree that my typed name and drawn signature have the same effect as a handwritten signature.
